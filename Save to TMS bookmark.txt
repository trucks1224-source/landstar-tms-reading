The "Save to TMS" bookmark for DAT
==================================

What it does: while you browse DAT postings, one click grabs every broker
email / company / lane visible on the page and saves them into the TMS
Prospects list (no screenshots needed). The TMS must be running on this
computer (Start TMS.bat).

Install once (Chrome/Edge):
1. Show the bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B).
2. Right-click the bookmarks bar -> Add page...
3. Name:  Save to TMS
4. URL:   paste the one line below
5. Save.

Use: on DAT, open a posting so the contact email shows, then click
"Save to TMS" in the bookmarks bar. A pop-up tells you how many brokers
were saved. They appear in TMS -> Tools -> Prospects, ready to email.

javascript:(async()=>{try{const r=await fetch('http://127.0.0.1:5173/api/prospects-import',{method:'POST',headers:{'Content-Type':'text/plain'},body:document.body.innerText});const j=await r.json();alert(j.error?'TMS: '+j.error:'TMS: saved '+j.saved+' new broker(s)'+(j.skipped?', '+j.skipped+' already saved':''))}catch(e){alert('TMS not reachable - is the TMS running on this computer?')}})()
