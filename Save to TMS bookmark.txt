The "Save to TMS" bookmark for DAT
==================================

What it does: while you browse DAT postings, one click grabs every broker
email / company / lane visible on the page and saves them into the TMS
Prospects list (no screenshots needed). The TMS must be running on this
computer (Start TMS.bat).

Install once (Chrome/Edge) - or update the bookmark you already have:
1. Show the bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B).
2. Right-click the bookmarks bar -> Add page...
   (to update: right-click your "Save to TMS" bookmark -> Edit...)
3. Name:  Save to TMS
4. URL:   paste the one line below
5. Save.

Use: on DAT, open a posting so the contact email shows, then click
"Save to TMS" in the bookmarks bar. A pop-up lists the new contacts saved
(emailed automatically) and the ones already in your list, with where each
stands (emailed, replied...). A contact counts as already saved only when
the same EMAIL is in your list: other people or offices at the same broker
are always saved and emailed. They appear in TMS -> Prospects.
