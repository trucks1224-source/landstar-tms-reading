Landstar TMS Reader

A transportation management application operated by Broski Transportation & Logistics.

What the application does

The TMS helps authorized users import and organize freight loads, manage offers and driver assignments, prepare Landstar posting files, and coordinate shipment updates and paperwork.

Gmail connection

With the account owner's permission, the TMS reads relevant Gmail messages and attachments to import load details, find customer and driver replies, and match shipment email threads. It can send operational emails on the connected account's behalf, including tracking reminders, ETA requests, customer updates, prospect correspondence, and paperwork. Some configured workflows send automatically; others require user approval.

Gmail access is optional and requires Google authorization. Signing into the TMS alone does not grant Gmail access. You can revoke access through your Google Account connections.
