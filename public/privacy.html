Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Landstar TMS Reader is operated by Broski Transportation & Logistics ("we"). This policy explains how we handle information in our transportation management application, including data accessed through a connected Google Gmail account.

Information we access and collect

When an account owner authorizes Gmail access, the application can read email messages and send email on that account's behalf. We use relevant email subjects, sender and recipient addresses, message bodies, thread identifiers, timestamps, and attachments such as rate confirmations, load details, and proof-of-delivery documents. We also obtain the connected account's email address and OAuth authorization tokens.

The TMS also handles information entered or imported by its users, including customer and prospect contact details, driver contact and equipment information, shipment routes, appointments, rates, tracking links, last-reported locations, and operational notes.

How information is used

We use this information to import and organize loads, match email threads to shipments, identify replies and update requests, and prepare or send operational communications. These communications include customer updates, driver tracking and ETA requests, prospect correspondence, paperwork, and load-inquiry notifications. Depending on the configured workflow, messages may require user approval or be sent automatically.

Gmail data is used only for TMS operations. We do not sell Gmail data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Landstar TMS Reader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage, access, and sharing

The application stores operational records in browser storage and in Supabase-hosted databases. Local automation workers may also keep queues, results, exports, and browser sessions on the computer running the bots. Gmail refresh tokens for server-side automation are stored in the server-side database; Google handles Google-account passwords.

Google provides Gmail and authorization services. Vercel hosts the deployed application and its server functions, and Supabase provides database and account services. These providers process information needed to deliver their services. Configured routing or rate services receive relevant shipment details when those features are used.

Authorized TMS users and users given access to shared customer workspaces can access the operational records available to them. Email contents and attachments are shared with the recipients of messages sent by the application, including copied recipients. Public load-inquiry links disclose the shipment details made available through those links. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law.

Human access to Google user data is limited to authorized operational use with the account owner's permission, or as necessary for security, legal compliance, and permitted support activities.

Security

We use HTTPS for hosted connections, authenticated workspace access, and server-side handling of automation credentials. Users should protect their account and the computers running the TMS: browser storage, downloaded exports, and local bot sessions may contain sensitive information. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Retention and deletion

We retain operational records until the operator deletes them. To request deletion of your information or the stored Gmail connection, contact trucks1224@gmail.com. We may need to verify your authority over the account or records. Copies already sent to email recipients or downloaded by other users are not removed by deleting TMS records. Backup copies may remain until the relevant provider's backup retention period ends; records required by law may be retained.

Your Google-account controls

You may revoke the application's Google access at any time through Google Account third-party connections. Revocation stops future authorized Gmail access once existing access expires or is invalidated. It does not automatically delete information already imported into the TMS. Contact us to request deletion of that information as well.

Changes and contact

We will publish changes on this page and update the effective date. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Broski Transportation & Logistics at trucks1224@gmail.com.
