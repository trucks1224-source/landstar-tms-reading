Open the TMS

Save to TMS bookmark

One click on a DAT posting saves the broker contacts to your Prospects list, and the TMS emails each of them from your Gmail.

1. Add the bookmark (once)

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: press Ctrl + Shift + B.
  2. Drag this green button up onto the bookmarks bar:
    Save to TMS

Can't drag it? Right-click the bookmarks bar → Add page…, name it Save to TMS, and paste this as the URL:

2. Connect your Gmail (once)

  1. Open the TMS and sign in.
  2. Click 📣 Prospects → Connect Gmail, pick your Gmail account and allow access.
  3. In Prospects → ⚙ Settings, fill in your name and phone. They sign your emails.

3. Use it

  1. On DAT, open a posting so the broker's email shows.
  2. Click Save to TMS in your bookmarks bar. A small window lists the contacts it found.
  3. Click Save. Every new contact gets its own intro email (several people at the same broker each get one), then one follow-up if nobody answers. Keep the TMS website open in a tab: it sends every few minutes.

If the small window doesn't open, allow pop-ups for DAT (the icon at the right end of the address bar).
